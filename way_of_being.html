WAYS OF BEING

700X80X24CM

STAINLESS STEEL

Shown at JAOU Biennale, Tunis

2024

WAYS OF BEING was the final artwork of RITUAL exhibition as part of JAOU Biennale. A streetlight with a changed archetype due to a simulated car accident that physically changed its axis, it morphed the object from an illuminating structure into a multi-purpose object: a playground for children, shade for Tunis’ cats, a place to sunbathe for the venue’s chickens, and a place to sit, discuss and be together. WAYS OF BEING allows us to see things differently through individual and collective transformation.
