WINDMADE RGB

210X297CM

PAPER, GLUE, TAPE

WOOD, WIND

Shanghai

2024

Windmade RGB is a square object comprised of iridescent paper. It featured in the video work “mistaking the rustling of leaves for the sound of water”, which, through dramatic reflections through the shifting rays of the sun, sought to introduce a new appreciation of a scene seen everyday by locals and offer a moment of meditation on natural beauty of the ancient town of Zhujiajiao.
